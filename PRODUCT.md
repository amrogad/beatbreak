# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who own audio files and want a playable rhythm-game chart without learning a chart editor. They play on desktop or touch devices, often in short sessions, and expect the song to drive the arrows.

## Product Purpose

Beatbreak turns an owned audio file into four difficulty charts and a playable four-lane game in the browser. It stores analysis, charts, scores and settings locally, with no account, upload, or server.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, precise, approachable, arcade-like. Clear over clever. Technical power is hidden behind simple choices.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered DAW or editor interfaces in the main play path
- Generic AI-looking gradients, glass cards, and identical card grids
- Over-explained calibration or timing controls
- Abrupt cuts that make the song ending feel broken

## Design Principles

- The song is the feedback; do not add noise that competes with it.
- Defaults should let a new player reach the results screen without configuration.
- Advanced controls stay behind explicit links, not in the main play path.
- Charts must stay deterministic unless the player explicitly asks for a new variation.
- Accessibility and reduced motion are first-class, not afterthoughts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard and touch play are both first-class.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Aim for WCAG AA contrast and visible focus states.
- No audio offset or timing calibration is exposed to the player.
